Data Deletion

Last updated: 24 September 2026

You can remove Movio's access to your Facebook data, or ask us to delete your data entirely. This page explains both.

1. Remove Movio's access in Movio

An agency owner or manager can disconnect a Facebook or Instagram account at any time:

  • Open Clients and choose the client.
  • Under Connected platforms, click Disconnect next to the page.

This deletes the stored access token and the analytics collected for that page. Movio can no longer reach the page.

2. Remove Movio from your Facebook account

  • In Facebook, open Settings & privacy, then Settings.
  • Go to Business integrations.
  • Find Movio, select it, and choose Remove.

3. Ask us to delete your data

To delete your Movio account and its data, email hello@area29labs.com from the address you use to sign in, with the subject Data deletion request, and include your agency name.

We will confirm your request within 7 days and delete your data within 30 days of confirming it. We may keep limited records where the law requires it, such as billing records. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

4. If you are a customer of an agency

If an agency that uses Movio holds your details — for example as a contact, or through comments on its page — that agency decides how your data is used. Please contact the agency first. If you cannot reach them, email us at hello@area29labs.com and we will help.
