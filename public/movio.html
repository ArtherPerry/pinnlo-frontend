Security & privacy

Your clients' data is handled like it's your own.

Agencies trust Movio with their clients' pages and audiences. Here is exactly what that means.

Scoped per client

Each client's pages, posts and contacts live in their own workspace. One client can never see or reach another's, and clients only ever see their own.

Isolated by design

Each agency and client lives in a separate, access-controlled workspace. Roles and permissions decide exactly who sees and does what.

You own your data

Your data stays yours. We never sell it and never use it to train models. Contacts export to CSV at any time.
