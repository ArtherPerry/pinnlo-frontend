1. Who we are

Movio ("Movio", "we", "us", "our") is a social media management platform operated by Area29Labs ("the Company"). Movio enables marketing agencies to manage social media accounts, content, and communications on behalf of their own clients.

Operator: Area29Labs, Vientiane, Laos

Product: Movio (moviosolution.com)

Privacy contact: hello@area29labs.com

2. Scope, and the important distinction between the two kinds of people whose data we handle

Movio is a business-to-business product. Understanding whose data is involved is essential to understanding this policy, because our responsibilities differ for each group.

2.1 Our customers (agencies and their staff)

Marketing agencies subscribe to Movio. Their owners, managers, and staff create accounts, log in, and use the platform. For this group, Movio is the party that decides how and why their account data is processed (in data-protection terms, the "controller").

2.2 The agency's clients (client-workspace users)

An agency's own clients may be given limited "client workspace" logins to review and approve posts. Their account data is handled similarly to agency staff.

2.3 Contacts and audience data uploaded by agencies

Agencies upload and store information about their clients' customers and audiences — names, email addresses, phone or WhatsApp numbers, tags, and notes. Agencies also connect social media accounts, through which Movio processes comments, messages, and audience insights from the connected platforms.

For this uploaded contact and audience data, the agency decides why and how it is processed, and Movio processes it on the agency's instructions. In data-protection terms, the agency is the "controller" and Movio is the "processor". This means the agency is primarily responsible for having a lawful basis to collect and use that data, and for honouring the rights of the individuals it concerns. Movio processes it only to provide the service.

3. What personal data we collect

3.1 From agencies and their staff/clients (we are controller)

  • Account details: name, email address, password (stored hashed), role, language preference.
  • Agency details: agency name, and business contact details you provide.
  • Usage and technical data: log-in times, IP address, device/browser information, and actions taken in the platform, kept for security and to operate the service.
  • Billing information: subscription tier and billing records. Movio does not currently take card payments in the app, so we do not store card numbers.

3.2 Uploaded by agencies about their clients' customers (we are processor)

  • Contact records: names, email addresses, phone numbers, WhatsApp identifiers, tags, and notes entered by the agency.
  • Social media data retrieved via connected accounts: comments and messages sent by the public to the client's connected pages, the sender's platform ID and public profile name, and aggregate page insights (follower counts, reach, engagement).
  • Content: posts, captions, images, and campaign material created or uploaded for publishing.

4. How and why we use personal data

We use personal data to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Movio platform and its features.
  • Authenticate users and keep accounts secure (including rate-limiting and abuse prevention).
  • Publish content and messages to connected social media accounts at the agency's direction.
  • Retrieve and display comments, messages, and analytics from connected accounts.
  • Provide the AI review feature, which analyses draft post content (and, where applicable, images) to give market-specific guidance.
  • Send email or messaging broadcasts that an agency configures to its own opted-in contacts.
  • Communicate with agencies about their account, service changes, and support.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

5. The AI review feature — specific disclosure

Movio's AI review feature sends the text of a draft post — and, where a publicly accessible image URL exists, the image — to a third-party AI provider (currently OpenAI, and/or Groq) to generate marketing guidance. The provider processes this content to return advice and does not, under our arrangement, use it to train their public models.

6. Content moderation — specific disclosure

Before a post enters the review workflow, Movio may check its text and images against a third-party moderation service (currently OpenAI) to screen for prohibited content. This check protects the platform and connected accounts.

7. Who we share personal data with (sub-processors)

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate Movio, each bound to process it only for that purpose:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) — to publish content, retrieve comments and messages, and obtain analytics, for accounts the agency connects.
  • Groq — to run the AI review feature on draft post content.
  • OpenAI — to check uploaded images against content-moderation rules.
  • Brevo — to deliver email campaigns that an agency sends.
  • DigitalOcean — cloud hosting and database storage.
  • Cloudflare — network delivery, security, and traffic routing.

8. Where data is stored and international transfer

Movio's application and database are hosted on DigitalOcean infrastructure.

9. How long we keep data

We keep account data while the agency's subscription is active. When an account closes, its data remains available for export for 30 days and is then deleted within 90 days, except for limited records we must keep to meet legal, billing, or dispute-resolution obligations. Contact and content data uploaded by an agency is kept until the agency deletes it or closes its account, and then follows the same timeline.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live and which law applies, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent.

For data an agency uploaded about you (contacts, audience data): the agency is the controller, so please contact the agency directly. Movio will assist the agency in responding.

For your Movio account data: contact us at hello@area29labs.com.

Data deletion: to request deletion of your data, contact hello@area29labs.com and we will process the request.

11. Marketing communications and broadcasts

Where an agency uses Movio to send email or messaging broadcasts, the agency is responsible for having a lawful basis and the recipients' consent. Movio provides unsubscribe and opt-in mechanisms and requires agencies to use them.

12. Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encryption of stored social media access tokens, hashed passwords, encrypted transport (HTTPS), access controls that isolate each agency's and client's data, and rate-limiting against abuse. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and to meet the security standards required of us.

13. Children

Movio is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the updated version and, where required, notify agencies of material changes.

15. Contact
